Frequently asked

My LED burned out — is that a bug?
No — that's the game. Every circuit is really simulated: too much current really does destroy parts, exactly like on a physical bench. Tap Reset and add a current-limiting resistor. The in-game Handbook (brass book button) has every component's ratings and the formulas to pick the right values.

I'm stuck on a level.
Tap the wand ("Solve it for me") — the tutor builds the solution one part at a time and explains each step with real numbers.

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. The whole game works offline, including in airplane mode.

Is my progress synced or uploaded anywhere?
No. Progress is stored only on your device. See the privacy policy.
