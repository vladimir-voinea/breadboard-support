Privacy Policy — Breadboard: Circuit Puzzles

Effective date: July 23, 2026

The short version

Breadboard collects no data. None. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect information. The game runs entirely on your device and works fully offline.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage data. The app makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress (which levels you've completed) is saved locally on your device using Apple's standard app storage. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children's privacy

Breadboard is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data at all, no data is collected from children.

Future changes

If a future update ever changes what the app does with data — for example, an optional advertising feature — this policy will be updated first, with the effective date above, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
